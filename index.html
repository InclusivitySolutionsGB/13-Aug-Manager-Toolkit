The Manager's Neurodiversity Toolkit

Bringing together current best practice around neurodiversity in the workplace — practical, human and built for managers.

⚠️
This is not a diagnostic tool

This toolkit brings together current understanding and best practice around neurodiversity in the workplace into an interactive resource for managers. It is designed to help you identify workplace challenges your team may be facing and to support you in making informed, practical decisions — but it cannot and does not diagnose any condition.

👤
You know your team

The traits and patterns you notice in a team member may be linked to a neurodivergent condition — but they may equally be the result of a significant life event, a change in circumstances, or something else entirely. As their manager, you are best placed to understand the full picture. This toolkit helps you make informed choices about how to support them, whatever the cause.

✅
No diagnosis needed to take action

Under the Equality Act 2010 and ACAS guidance, a formal diagnosis is not required before you can offer support. What matters is that someone is experiencing a barrier at work — and that you respond to it thoughtfully. This toolkit will help you do exactly that.

🚀
Better support means better outcomes for everyone

When managers take the time to understand and respond to the different ways people work, whole teams benefit. Inclusive practices don’t just help the individual — they improve communication, reduce stress and create a working environment where everyone can do their best work.
